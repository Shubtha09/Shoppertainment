01

Experiential Technology

Engaging your customers like never before.

01 Immersive Experiences

  • Virtual Reality: bring the experience to the user with fully immersive environments when physical visits are not possible.
  • Augmented Reality: blend digital components into the real world so users interact with virtual content while still distinguishing between the two.
  • Mixed Reality: physical and digital objects coexisting and interacting in real time through MR glasses and headsets.
  • Holographic displays for launches, showrooms, and stage moments.

02 Interactive Installations

  • Gesture-based solutions: interactive video walls and displays that react to the user, making the experience dynamic.
  • Brain wave reader: participate using the power of your mind, controlling car races or tug-of-war through concentration levels.
  • Sensors and triggers: controlled LEDs, sensors, and triggers that create campaigns beyond novelty.

03 Digital Engagement

  • Browser games: HTML5 games with no app download, accessible via URL, SMS, or QR code for frictionless digital engagement.
  • Interactive videos: questions, hotspots, and lead generation layers that turn viewing into an active experience.

04 Photobooths and Effects

  • AR filters: gesture-controlled events, in-camera AR games, 3D objects, and facial expression filters.
  • AI photobooths: AI-driven capture that personalises and enhances user photos with real-time effects and augmented overlays.
